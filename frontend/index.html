Why Echo? — Architecture Resilience Intelligence

Understanding silent cascading failure propagation before deploying to production

Modern distributed software systems fail silently through multi-hop cascade effects—a single third-party payment gateway timeout or a session cache eviction quickly ripples upstream across order queues, gateways, and checkout funnels before traditional APM alarms can isolate root causes. Echo solves this by acting as a proactive architectural twin: it maintains an exact directed dependency graph of your microservices, datastores, endpoints, and third-party APIs. Natural language questions trigger instant, in-memory graph clones in FalkorDB where faults and library bumps are simulated with zero production risk, calculating the exact blast radius, downstream caller impact, and registered failover routes.
