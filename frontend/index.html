What do these numbers mean?
Blast radius
The share of paths in your dependency graph that stop working when the target fails. Higher means more of the system is affected.
Tier-1 down
Core services on the critical path, like payments, checkout and auth, that are fully unavailable. These stop revenue.
Tier-2 and 3 degraded
Downstream services that keep running but lose a dependency. Expect slower responses, partial features or retries.
Broken APIs
Public HTTP endpoints that rely on the failed component and will return errors to customers or partners.

    How Echo works

    Distributed systems rarely fail where they break. A payment timeout or a cache eviction ripples through order queues, gateways and checkout before your alerts can name a cause. Echo keeps a directed dependency graph of your services, datastores, endpoints and third-party APIs. Ask a question in plain language and it clones the graph in memory, injects the fault, and returns the blast radius, downstream impact and registered failover routes. Nothing touches production.
